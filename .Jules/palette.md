## 2025-05-14 - [Accordion for Medical Disclaimers]
**Learning:** Medical applications often have long disclaimers that take up significant vertical space "above the fold", pushing interactive elements down. Using a collapsed gr.Accordion for these disclaimers improves the initial focus on the main task (e.g., image upload) while keeping the legal information accessible.
**Action:** Always wrap long secondary text or disclaimers in collapsible gr.Accordion components (defaulting to open=False) to maintain a clean and focused interface.
